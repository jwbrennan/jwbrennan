# Joseph Brennan

## Senior Technical Consultant - Wolfram Research

I took an interest in programming while studying astrophysics at the University of St Andrews.
My thesis was related to gravitational microlensing, specifically the extreme low-mass limits of what we might able to detect with next-generation extremely large telescopes (ELTs) by exploiting the finite-source effect for sources like white dwarfs. My supervisor was Dr. Martin Dominik.

- 🌍  I'm based in Oxfordshire, UK
